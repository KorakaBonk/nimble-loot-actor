## 1.0.0

First public release.

- New **Loot** actor type for the Nimble system, with a sheet in Nimble's own style.
- GMs fill loot with objects (dragged from the Items directory, a compendium or a character sheet) and gold, silver and copper.
- Players click a loot token to open it, take objects into their character, take coins, or split the coins evenly among the party.
- The GM can hand objects and coins from the sheet straight to a character.
- Every take, hand-out and split can be announced in chat (world setting, on by default), in Nimble's chat card style. Players' actions are spoken by their character, the GM's by the loot actor.
- When Nimble Party Loot 2.1 or newer is active, objects and coins can be moved from a loot pile into the party loot.
- All transfers are carried out by the GM's client, so players never need ownership of the loot actor.
